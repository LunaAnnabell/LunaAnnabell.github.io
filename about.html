About Me

Designing at the intersection of technology, people, and expression.

I am Luna Annabell Linné Jensen, an MSc graduate in Digitalisation and Application Development with a background in Art and Technology. My work combines system development, human-computer interaction, physical computing, and creative practice.

How I Work

I enjoy translating complex technical possibilities into experiences that feel understandable, purposeful, and human. My process moves between research, prototyping, implementation, and reflection, with attention to both how a system works and how it feels to use.

Across my projects, I have worked with conversational AI, mobile and web applications, interactive installations, sensors, projection, and responsive physical systems. I am especially interested in projects where technical development and creative exploration strengthen each other.
