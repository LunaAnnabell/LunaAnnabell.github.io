Personal Art Practice

Building, drawing, and bringing different worlds together.

In my personal practice, I spend time exploring details in everything from physical sculpture to drawing. This work is where my creative and technical ways of thinking meet most naturally.

Careful Making

I draw hyperrealistic portraits because the process gives me a sense of calm and focus. Working with texture and the way light moves across an eye has taught me to analyse structures and patterns while keeping sight of the whole image.

It is a patient process, and the satisfaction comes from seeing a detailed result grow through sustained attention.

The Supporting Structure

When I build sculptures, I work with form, space, and aesthetics. Although the focus may be on the outside, the internal structure is what makes the finished expression possible.

From the first framework to the final surface, each step and material choice contributes to an object that can function in a space and carry itself physically and conceptually.

Two Worlds Becoming One

I am fascinated by how different materials and fields can be mixed and brought together. Light, sound, movement, and physical elements can build a bridge between otherwise separate worlds.

The technology is not the goal by itself. What matters is how the combined result creates an experience that can give something to its audience.
