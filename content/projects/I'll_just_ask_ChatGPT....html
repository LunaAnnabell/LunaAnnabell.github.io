Master's Thesis / Human-Computer Interaction / Conversational AI

I'll just ask ChatGPT

Conversational AI as a sense-making tool for physical and mental well-being

Role: Co-author, Qualitative Researcher, Reflexive Thematic Data Analyst

Context: 10. semester Thesis project at Aalborg University

Timeframe: Fall 2025

Description: The study explores the informal role general-purpose chatbots play in health-related information-seeking and well-being management. It addresses the empirical gap surrounding how users formulate concerns, prompt models, and make sense of responses when dealing with unstructured health queries.

Solution and Conclusion

Conclusion: The research methodology comprised 20 semi-structured qualitative interviews featuring a scenario-based chatbot task (evaluating physical symptoms vs. mental well-being scenarios) analyzed using Reflexive Thematic Analysis. The findings generated four core themes: Available and Low-Friction Use, Conversational AI as a Sense-Making Tool, User Agency, and Social Contracts and Privacy. The thesis concludes that chatbots primarily function as low-effort sense-making tools while introducing tensions regarding trust, verification, agreeableness, and responsibility.
