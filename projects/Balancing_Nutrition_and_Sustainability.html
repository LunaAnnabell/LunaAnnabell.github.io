P7 Student Project / Web Solution Development

Balancing Nutrition and Sustainability

A Tool for informed Dietary Choices

Role: Project Team Member

Context: P7 project at Aalborg University (Institute of Computer Science) / Supervisor: Dimitrios Raptis

Duration: 1st October 2024 – 20th December 2024 (fall 2024)

Elevator Pitch: Young Danes face a disconnect between wanting to eat sustainably and lacking the concrete knowledge to balance environmental impacts with proper nutrition. To address this, the project developed a web-based application that integrates a food database ("Den Store Klimadatabase") to let users track macros, calories, and CO2 emissions through visualisations and feedback comparisons.

2. Problem & Objective

The Challenge: Young Danes consume unhealthy patterns (frequently missing fruit/vegetable intake targets) and lack adequate knowledge about sustainable food choices, despite reporting high climate concerns.

Target Audience & Stakeholders: Young Danes aged 18–29 seeking to harmonize dietary health with climate-friendly consumption.

3. Methods & Approach

Development Process: Applied Computational Thinking (Decomposition, Pattern Recognition, Abstraction, and Algorithmic Design) alongside Wizard of Oz (WOZ) prototyping to shape core features.

Analysis & Research: Leveraged related literature, CONCITO climate surveys, linear programming concepts, and datasets from "Den Store Klimadatabase".

4. Technical & Visual Foundation

Tech Stack: Python with Flask (Backend), SQLite3 (Database), and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (Frontend utilizing Google Charts).

Tools & Architecture: Structured modular folders separating templates, static files, and database table creation scripts (createtables.py).

5. Solution & Artifacts

User Authentication, Search & Calculation Engine, Visualizations & Feedback via Google Charts, and dedicated Transparency Page.

6. Results & Key Learnings

Impact & Feedback: Successfully implemented core functional requirements (user login, food searches, calculations, visual charts, and emission comparisons), bridging complex environmental data into a user-friendly format.
