Art and Technology - Bachelor Project

Beneath The Weight

A dynamic sculpture designed for the climate festival Aalborg2024

Role: Art and Technology Bachelor Student

Context: Art and Technology Bachelor Project at Aalborg University

Duration: 21/02/2024 – 31/05/2024 (spring 2024)

Elevator Pitch: This project explores the intersection of environmental sustainability and art through "Beneath The Weight," a life-sized dynamic sculpture created to evoke emotional and intellectual responses to climate change. Combining recycled materials, moving hardware, and an intense soundscape, the sculpture serves as a medium for public engagement and discourse on sustainable production in the art world.

2. Problem & Objective

The Challenge: While climate change is a pressing societal issue, traditional information often lacks emotional resonance, and the art world itself faces a paradox regarding the carbon footprint and material sustainability of producing and transporting large-scale artworks.

Target Audience & Stakeholders: Festival attendees and exhibition goers at Aalborg2024 (exhibited at CREATE, AAU), alongside local partners like Aalborg Kommune and Nordværk.

3. Methods & Approach

Development Process: Iterative design guided by the theoretical frameworks of John Dewey (Art as Experience) and Christian Jantzen (Experiences and experiencing: a psychological framework), focusing on doing/undergoing, Erfahren (accumulated knowledge), and Erleben (present sensing and feeling).

Analysis & Research: Walk-along interviews conducted with participants during the exhibition (May 16–17, 2024), categorized through descriptive, linguistic, and conceptual coding.

4. Technical & Visual Foundation

Tech Stack: Arduino, Processing, and Open Sound Control (OSC) library for network communication.

Tools & Architecture: An M5Stack, ATOM Motion, a BNO055 orientation sensor, an H-bridge (BB-VNH3SP30), a 12V linear actuator, and a 3-minute layered audio soundscape mapped to motor movement via amplitude analysis.

5. Solution & Artifacts

Physical life-sized sculpture built from recycled materials with dynamic movement and sound integration via PID-controlled motor actuation.

6. Results & Key Learnings

Impact & Feedback: Walk-along interviews revealed that the sculpture effectively provoked sensory and emotional reactions, stimulating intellectual reflections and community discussions regarding sustainability.

Personal Takeaways: Demonstrated that transparency, conscious material reduction, and purposeful recycling partnerships add profound value to art and technology installations.
