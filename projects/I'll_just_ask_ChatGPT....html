Masters Thesis / Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)

"I'll just ask ChatGPT..."

Conversational AI as a sense-making tool for physical and mental well-being

Role: Master's Thesis Author

Context: Master's thesis at Aalborg University (Institute of Computer Science) / Supervisor: Niels van Berkel

Duration: February -June 2026 (spring 2026)

Elevator Pitch: This study investigates how people experience and use conversational AI when managing everyday physical and mental well-being concerns. Based on 20 semi-structured interviews and a scenario-based chatbot task analysed using reflexive thematic analysis, the study reveals that chatbots function as low-friction, accessible sense-making tools that help users organize uncertain symptoms and thoughts into manageable explanations and next steps.

2. Problem & Objective

The Challenge: While conversational AI is increasingly used for everyday well-being concerns (such as sleep issues, stress, or unclear symptoms), relatively little is known about how people experience these interactions in practice. Using chatbots for health introduces risks and responsibilities regarding response evaluation, trust, and ethical complexity.

Target Audience & Stakeholders: Non-professional users seeking informal guidance or reassurance for physical and mental well-being concerns alongside or before consulting other sources.

3. Methods & Approach

Development Process: A qualitative study design comprising a pre-interview questionnaire (including the AIAS-4 attitude scale), a semi-structured interview, a scenario-based chatbot interaction task using ChatGPT, and a follow-up reflection.

Analysis & Research: Reflexive Thematic Analysis (RTA) following Braun and Clarke's six phases, supported by individual researcher journals and study guides.

4. Technical & Visual Foundation

Tech Stack: ChatGPT used via shared project folders for the scenario tasks; Whisper / NoScribe and NoScribe Editor used for local audio transcription.

Tools & Architecture: Qualitative coding executed using Microsoft Word comments aggregated into Microsoft Excel via a Visual Basic (VBA) script.

5. Solution & Artifacts

Four Analytical Themes: Available and Low-Friction Use, Conversational AI as a Sense-Making Tool, User Agency, and Social Contracts and Privacy.

6. Results & Key Learnings

Impact & Feedback: Participants valued chatbots for speed, availability, privacy, and flexibility, but also faced tensions regarding verification, over-agreeableness (sycophancy), responsibility, and trust.

Personal Takeaways: Conversational AI should not be viewed merely as an information source, but as an active sense-making partner that shapes how users understand and act upon their health uncertainties.
